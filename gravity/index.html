🌍 The takeaway:
Gravity is a pull between any two objects with mass. It only pulls, it never pushes, and it works without touching. More mass means a stronger pull. Farther apart means a weaker pull, and the pull drops off fast: doubling the distance cut it to one fourth. Earth pulls on you because Earth has an enormous mass. The Moon has less mass than Earth, so its pull is weaker: on the Moon your weight would drop, but your mass would stay exactly the same.
